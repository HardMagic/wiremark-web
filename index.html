Visual-first Questions, answered

Everything you
want to know

Contact us
  • 01.
    Which repositories can I connect?
    WireMark connects GitHub, GitLab, or a local repository. Repositories stay read-only until you accept the detected execution plan, then work directly over the live canvas.
  • 02.
    Is my code safe in previews?
    Yes. Every preview runs sandboxed with no access to your system. Repositories remain read-only until trust is established, credentials stay encrypted, and telemetry is off by default.
  • 03.
    How does the visual loop work?
    The loop is observe, mark up, express intent, implement, compare, verify, then approve or revert. Every change is attributable and reversible, so you can always step back and redo.
  • 04.
    Can I experiment with visual source alternatives?
    Yes. WireMark creates app-owned Git worktrees from a clean named branch, so you can explore visual source alternatives side by side, compare the evidence, and promote only the option you approve.
  • 05.
    When is WireMark generally available?
    WireMark is available today. Start free on the Starter plan and upgrade as your team grows.
