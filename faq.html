  • 01.
    Is WireMark a replacement for my code editor?
    WireMark is designed around the running product as the primary surface. Monaco, files, Git, the terminal, and raw logs live in the secondary Implementation workspace when you need them.
  • 02.
    How does workspace trust work?
    WireMark reviews the execution plan before you build, fingerprints change trust, and keeps credentials in OS-backed encrypted storage.
  • 03.
    What is the observe-to-verify loop?
    Observe the live canvas, mark up what should change, express intent, implement, compare, and verify in the browser — then approve or revert.
  • 04.
    Which providers are supported?
    Today WireMark connects GitHub, GitLab, and local repositories. New providers require a product decision and production OAuth design.
  • 05.
    When will WireMark be generally available?
    Start free on the Starter plan and upgrade as your team grows.
