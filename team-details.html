Build the future of visual-first development

WireMark is built by a small, senior team at HardMagic. We obsess over the loop — observe, mark up, express intent, implement, compare, verify — and make every change attributable and reversible.

  • Focus: Visual workspace & verification
  • Status: Private beta
  • Email: hello@wiremark.net

More about the role

Founding engineers here own a surface end to end — from the annotation engine in packages/core to the Electron main-process trust and verification layers. We work slowly, verify everything in the browser, and never ship a change without evidence.

If that sounds like you, we are always looking for senior engineers who care about trust, privacy, and the craft of building. Email hello@wiremark.net.
