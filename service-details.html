The visual loop,
in depth.

WireMark connects GitHub, GitLab, or a local repository and turns it into a live canvas — observe, mark up, verify, and ship with confidence.

Work over the
live canvas

WireMark is built around a single contract: the running product is the primary surface. Observe it, mark it up, express intent, and WireMark maps your change back to the source behind it.

Monaco, files, Git, the terminal, and raw logs live in the secondary Implementation workspace — they appear when you need them, not before.

Locked selectors

Set authoritative selectors so experiments stay honest — a DOM-only change is not a completed source change.

Source-ranked candidates

When you mark up the product, WireMark surfaces ranked source candidates with honest attribution.

Attributable and reversible

Every edit carries a visual target, ranked candidates, and a reversible change set — with before/after evidence included.

Verified in the browser

Locked selectors stay authoritative. Every change is checked at the viewports that matter, and nothing ships without evidence.

Approve or revert — the choice is always yours, and the working tree stays clean while you experiment.
